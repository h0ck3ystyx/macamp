# MacAmp privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

MacAmp is an offline audio player for files you choose on your Mac. MacAmp does not create an account, show advertising, track you, or collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

MacAmp accesses audio files, playlists, and skin packages only after you choose them through macOS or drag them into the app. Security-scoped bookmarks may be stored locally so MacAmp can reopen those items after relaunch. Playback state, playlists, equalizer settings, visualization settings, window layout, and skin preferences are stored locally in MacAmp's sandboxed application container. You can remove that local information by clearing playlists in the app or deleting the app's container.

MacAmp does not include analytics, telemetry, third-party advertising, or network-service SDKs. The app does not send your filenames, audio, metadata, artwork, playlists, settings, or usage activity to the developer or any third party.

Apple may process App Store purchases, downloads, and optional diagnostic information under Apple's own policies and your device settings. That processing is not performed by MacAmp.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the MacAmp support page at <https://github.com/h0ck3ystyx/macamp/issues>.

If MacAmp's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.
