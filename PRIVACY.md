# MioAmp privacy policy

Effective September 29, 2026

MioAmp is an offline audio player for files you choose on your Mac. MioAmp does not create an account, show advertising, track you, or collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

MioAmp accesses audio files, playlists, and skin packages only after you choose them through macOS or drag them into the app. Security-scoped bookmarks may be stored locally so MioAmp can reopen those items after relaunch. Playback state, playlists, equalizer settings, visualization settings, window layout, and skin preferences are stored locally in MioAmp's sandboxed application container. You can remove that local information by clearing playlists in the app or deleting the app's container.

MioAmp does not include analytics, telemetry, third-party advertising, or network-service SDKs. The app does not send your filenames, audio, metadata, artwork, playlists, settings, or usage activity to the developer or any third party.

Apple may process App Store purchases, downloads, and optional diagnostic information under Apple's own policies and your device settings. That processing is not performed by MioAmp.

Questions about this policy can be submitted through the MioAmp support page at <https://github.com/h0ck3ystyx/mioamp/issues>.

If MioAmp's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before the changed version is released.
