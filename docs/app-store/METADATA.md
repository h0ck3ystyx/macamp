## Promotional text

Make your local music library feel personal with a compact modular player, customizable skins, a ten-band equalizer, playlists, and live visualizations.

## Description

MioAmp is a compact, customizable audio player for the music files on your Mac.

Build a playlist by opening files or folders, then reorder tracks with drag and drop. MioAmp reads embedded titles when available, restores your queue between launches, and can import and export M3U, M3U8, and PLS playlists.

Shape playback with a ten-band equalizer and presets. Open the live visualization window, choose from six original effects, and adjust its sensitivity. Arrange the player, playlist, equalizer, and visualization as separate modules, or switch to a compact player when you need more room.

Choose a bundled skin or create and share your own MioAmp skin package. Interface scaling, keyboard controls, media keys, and accessible control labels keep the player practical as well as personal.

Supported audio includes MP3, AAC, M4A, ALAC, FLAC up to 192 kHz, WAV, AIFF, Ogg Vorbis, and Opus. MioAmp plays local files entirely on your Mac. It has no account, ads, analytics, tracking, or cloud upload.

Requires macOS 14 or later.
